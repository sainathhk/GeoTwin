3 · Flight camera viewpoints

Bundled DJI0004 locations will load automatically; a matching SRT/CSV can replace them.
Each view starts from a GPS/altitude sample and looks toward the model. DJI SRT does not contain camera orientation, so the viewing direction is approximate. This shows the flight position, not an exact replay of what the lens saw.

4 · Add geographic landmarks

Place a pin on a visible roof/footprint point. It records WGS84 latitude/longitude and local Z in the exported GeoJSON.

5 · Fill visible mesh holes

After loading, gaps inside the visible model footprint are automatically filled with the mean adjacent surface color and marked in amber. This is a view-only inferred overlay: the OBJ/GLB geometry itself is not changed, and filled areas are not camera-observed.

Optional · place an inferred 3D patch

The manual amber patch is a separate, flat 3D placeholder. Click a visible surface to place it at that height, or click empty space to use fallback Z. It is never counted as measured mesh.

6 · Review and export

GeoJSON contains point landmarks and inferred patch footprints with status metadata. Keep it with the GLB; the GLB alone does not encode this local WGS84 transform.
